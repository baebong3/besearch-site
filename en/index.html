Process

Five steps from inquiry to delivery.

  1. Inquiry and materialTell us what you need and what you have; we review and reply.
  2. Scope and quoteWe agree on length, formats, schedule and rounds of revision, and send a quote.
  3. DraftYou see the outline and a sample screen first, so we agree on direction.
  4. Review and revisionSend your comments together; we apply them within the agreed rounds.
  5. DeliveryFiles in the agreed formats, or a link to the screen.

FAQ

What should I send?

Send what you have as it is: spreadsheets, statistical tables, survey results or interview transcripts. For a proposal, include the tender notice, the RFP and a template if there is one. If the material is not yet tidy, we will review it and tell you whether we can work with it.

Which formats can I get?

Familiar formats (HWPX, PPT, DOCX), new formats (HTML, PDF) and web dashboards. The same content can be delivered in two or more formats.

How are price and schedule decided?

They depend on length, formats, whether we also write the content, and the rounds of revision. Tell us about your material and the output you want; once the scope is set we send a quote and a schedule.

How many revisions are included?

The number of rounds is agreed with the quote. One round means one consolidated set of comments.

How is my material handled?

We use it only for the work you ordered. Unpublished material and deliverables are not disclosed or used as samples without your consent. See our Privacy Policy (Korean).
